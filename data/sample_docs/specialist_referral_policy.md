# Specialist Referral Policy (Fictional Sample: Northwind Health Plan)

Members enrolled in an HMO plan need a referral from their primary care provider (PCP) before seeing most specialists. PPO members do not need a referral, but they pay less when they see in-network specialists.

A referral is valid for 90 days from the date it is issued and covers up to six visits with the named specialist. If the member needs more visits, the PCP must submit a referral extension before the original referral expires.

Referrals are not required for OB/GYN annual exams, in-network behavioral health visits, or emergency care. Urgent care visits also do not require a referral.

If a referral is submitted to a specialist who is no longer in network, the referral is returned to the PCP's office with the reason code NETWORK_INACTIVE. The member should be helped to choose another in-network specialist within the same specialty.
