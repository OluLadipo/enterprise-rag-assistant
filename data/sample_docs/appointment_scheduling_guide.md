# Appointment Scheduling Guide (Fictional Sample: Northwind Health Plan)

Routine primary care appointments should be offered within 10 business days. Urgent but non-emergency appointments should be offered within 48 hours.

When scheduling with a specialist, confirm that an active referral is on file for HMO members before booking. If no referral exists, offer to send a message to the member's PCP requesting one.

Members can reschedule up to 24 hours before an appointment without a missed-visit flag. Two missed visits within 12 months may lead to outreach from a care coordinator.

For behavioral health appointments, representatives may share the behavioral health line number so members can schedule directly. Behavioral health visits with in-network providers do not require a referral.
